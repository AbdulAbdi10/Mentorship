A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Curious?
That’s a good start.

Who is this for?

High school and college students who want guidance, and professionals interested in sharing career insights with the next generation.

What can I talk about with a mentor?

Start with your interests, questions about careers or college, and skills you want to develop. A mentor can share a perspective from their own experience.

Do I need to know my future career?

No. Curiosity is a great starting point. Exploring interests and discovering new possibilities is part of the journey.

How do I register?

Choose the student or mentor button to open our Google registration form in a new tab. Both buttons use the same form. Follow the instructions in the form to submit your interest.

What happens to the information I submit?

Registration happens on Google Forms. Review the information and any privacy or consent instructions provided there before submitting. This website itself does not collect registration details.
